        Welcome to Tectonics

        Plate boundaries and hot spots as 3D block diagrams you can play, scrub and slice open.

        • Play millions of years. Press play, or drag the timeline to scrub through each boundary.
        • Turn it, slice it. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and move the cut to look inside.
        • Learn with video. Mike’s lessons sit in the panel, each with a button that sets up the model.
        • Make a worksheet. New numbers every time, with an answer key.
        • Hold it. Save an STL to print the block, or a USDZ to see it in AR.

        Tip: press ? any time for keyboard shortcuts.
